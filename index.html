Two paths to earn points
AIM rewards two different kinds of people in two different ways. Both end up with phantom points, but how those points come into your hands is different.
Employees
Granted upfront, vests over time. When you join, you receive a phantom grant. You earn ownership of those points over 4 years (with a 1-year cliff). Salary covers your work day-to-day; phantom equity is your share of long-term company value.
Advisors / Contributors
Earned per outcome, no upfront grant. AIM signs an earning agreement listing what you can earn for each outcome. Points are awarded only when an outcome happens, and they're 100% yours immediately. No vesting, no waiting.

How the math works
Every payout follows the same formula. Three steps.
Step 1 — The 10% pool
AIM has set aside 10% of company value as a phantom pool. That 10% is divided into 10,000 points, so each point = 0.001% of AIM's value.
Step 2 — Multiply by exit value
When AIM is acquired or has a payout event, your owned points convert to cash at AIM's value at that moment.
Step 3 — Apply ownership
Employees: ownership = vested %. You earn ownership over 4 years.
Advisors / Contributors: ownership = 100% the moment the outcome is verified. Points awarded are immediately yours.
Payout = Owned Points × 0.001% × Exit Value

Example: 100 owned points, AIM exits at £100M
= 100 × 0.001% × £100M
= £100,000

Rules & process

The foundational rules and the approval process for every grant or earning agreement.

Core rules
What earns phantom points
Phantom points reward measurable contributions that materially increase AIM's long-term company value. Examples below are illustrative. Anything that genuinely moves AIM forward in a sustained way is on the table — judged on impact, not on counts or hours.
Building things that ship. Prototypes, features, integrations, infrastructure that go into production and get used by clients.
Sustained delivery, not one-off help. Real ongoing involvement in execution where you actually move work forward.
Validated outcomes. Work that produced measurable retention, efficiency, or revenue impact.
Sustained pipeline of business. Bringing in meaningful long-term revenue through ongoing relationship work, not single intros.
Strategic channel access. Opening NHS, council frameworks, large care groups, or other major buyer channels at scale.
Technical defensibility. Building parts of AIM's product moat — AI engine, integrations, scheduling, compliance automation.
Operational scale. Removing founder bottlenecks, building processes, making AIM run independently.
Demos and sales execution. Actively running demos, closing meetings, project-managing pilots through to contract.
What does NOT earn phantom points
Ideas without execution. AI can generate ideas. AIM rewards delivery, not speculation.
One-off conversations. Single intros or pieces of advice with no measurable outcome or follow-through.
Single small deals already covered by Deal Partner commission. A one-off intro is paid via commission only — see note below.
Promises that haven't materialised. Future commitments don't earn points. Outcomes do.
Work fully covered by salary or commission, unless the impact is materially exceptional and produces lasting company value.
Deal Partner Programme overlap. The Deal Partner Programme pays per-deal commission for short-term revenue. Phantom equity rewards long-term company value on top of that. A single deal earns commission. Building a sustained pipeline, opening a major channel, or driving enterprise expansion as a deal partner can earn phantom points in addition to commission. The bigger the long-term value created, the more phantom points are on the table — judged on actual impact, not deal counts.
Dilution & pool changes
What is dilution? When AIM issues new equity (for example, raising investment), everyone's percentage share of the company gets slightly smaller, even though the total company value usually grows. The phantom pool can be affected the same way.
While AIM is bootstrapped or pre-Series A: the 10% phantom pool is fully protected. No dilution applies.
From a Series A or equivalent priced round onward: the pool may dilute pro rata alongside other shareholders. Founders may, at their discretion, top up the pool from new issuances to maintain the 10% target.
Already-awarded points are always preserved. Once points are awarded to you (employee grant or advisor/contributor agreement outcome), they cannot be reduced by future dilution.
Only the unallocated pool and the size of future grants are affected by any dilution event.
Full legal terms covering dilution, exit events, tax treatment, leaver provisions, and clawback are set out in the signed grant or earning agreement. This UI summary is for clarity, not the binding contract.
Approval process

Your vesting schedule

4-year vesting with a 1-year cliff. As an employee, you get a grant on day one and earn ownership of it over time.

Understanding vesting in plain English
Vesting means the percentage of your grant that you have actually earned and own at any moment in time. You don't get all your points the day you join — you earn them gradually as you stay with AIM. If you leave early, you keep what has vested. Anything unvested goes back to the pool.
The cliff is the first 12 months. If you leave before reaching 12 months, you walk away with 0%. The cliff exists so people who join and leave quickly don't get phantom equity for short stints.

Earning agreements

How advisors and contributors earn phantom points. No upfront grant, no vesting — points are earned per outcome and immediately yours.

Why this is different from employees. Employees get a grant on day one and earn ownership over 4 years (vesting). Advisors and contributors don't — there's no employment relationship to gate. Instead, you sign an earning agreement that lists what you can earn for each verified outcome. Points are awarded only when an outcome happens, and they're 100% yours immediately. No vesting math, no waiting period.
How it works in 4 steps
1
Sign an earning agreement
AIM and you sign a written agreement listing the outcomes you can earn points for, with a point value next to each one. The agreement is tailored to what you're actually doing — intros, technical work, strategic support, or a mix.
2
Do the work
Make the intro, build the prototype, run the demo, drive the strategy. Whatever's in your agreement.
3
Outcome verified by founders
When an outcome from your agreement actually happens, founders verify it. The corresponding points are awarded into your name.
4
Points are 100% yours immediately
No waiting period, no vesting clock. From the moment they're awarded they're owned. They convert to cash at the next AIM payout event.
Track 1 — Strategic introductions & channel access
For advisors and partners who open doors to clients, councils, NHS bodies, or large care groups. Points awarded follow the deal, not the conversation. This is also how Deal Partner activity earns phantom equity when it scales beyond single deals into sustained pipeline or major channel access.

Worked example. Suppose AIM and you sign a Track 1 agreement worth up to 200 points. You make warm intros that land meetings — 10% of 200 = 20 points awarded immediately. The opportunity becomes qualified — another 15% added (cumulative 25%) = 50 points total. The deal closes — 25% more added = 100 points total. You then build a sustained pipeline — full 100% earned = 200 points total. At a £100M exit, those 200 points are worth £200,000. If the deal had stalled at "qualified", you'd own 50 points = £50,000.
Important rules
1
Each agreement is tailored to one track at sign-off, with specific outcomes and point values written down.
2
Founders verify each outcome before any points are awarded. No self-certification.
3
No outcome means no points, full stop. Time alone never earns anything.
4
Agreements expire after 24 months unless extended in writing. Outcomes hit during the agreement period are kept; future ones are not.
5
Points awarded are 100% yours immediately. There is no vesting, no cliff, no waiting period.
6
Ideas alone don't qualify. AIM can generate ideas with AI. Execution, delivery, and validated outcomes earn points.
7
Founders judge scale on actual impact, not generic counts. A single major channel win can outweigh many smaller deals.
